Payroll and Compensation Policy
SAMPLE POLICY - fictional content for the OnboardIQ demo. Not real company policy.

Pay schedule
Salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available in the HR portal under Payroll, two working days before the payment date.

New joiners
Employees who join on or before the twentieth day of a month are paid for that partial month in the same payroll cycle. Employees who join after the twentieth receive their first pro-rated salary in the following month's payroll.

Bank and tax details
Submit your salary bank account details and tax declaration during onboarding. Missing details can delay your first payment.

Reimbursements
Approved business expenses submitted by the fifteenth of the month are reimbursed with that month's salary.
