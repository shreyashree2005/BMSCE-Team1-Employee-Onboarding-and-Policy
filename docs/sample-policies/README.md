# Sample policy documents

Fictional policies for trying the Documents page and semantic search. They are **not** real company policy.

| File | Format | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `leave-and-attendance-policy.pdf` | PDF, 3 pages | Annual and sick leave, public holidays, carry forward (tests page numbers) |
| `information-security-policy.docx` | DOCX with headings | Passwords, phishing, devices (tests section metadata) |
| `payroll-and-compensation-policy.txt` | TXT | Pay schedule, new joiners, reimbursements |
| `hybrid-work-policy.txt` | TXT | Remote work eligibility and expectations |

Try searches such as "How much vacation time do I get?", "Someone emailed me a weird link asking for my login" or "When do new joiners get their first salary?".
