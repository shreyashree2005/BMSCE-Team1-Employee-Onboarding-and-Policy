Hybrid Work Policy
SAMPLE POLICY - fictional content for the OnboardIQ demo. Not real company policy.

Eligibility
Employees who have completed their first thirty days of service may work remotely up to two days per week, with their manager's approval.

During the first thirty days, new hires are expected to work from the office so they can settle in with their team.

Expectations
Remote days must be agreed with your manager and recorded in the HR portal. Teams may set common in-office days for collaboration. Employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours when working remotely.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and headset. A one-time home office allowance is available after ninety days of service.
